About

My name is Victor Ronin. I'm a second-degree black belt in Tang Soo Do, training in New York City under my instructor, Master Ralph. Our studio is part of the World Tang Soo Do Association.

One part of training I've come to really enjoy is learning forms.

This is a personal project to learn and document 100 Tang Soo Do–adjacent forms. That number may shift over time, but the goal is long-term consistency and progress — posting these videos is how I hold myself accountable and keep moving forward.

As many practitioners know, a form can be refined indefinitely; some spend years, even decades, polishing a single hyung. Because of that, my standard here isn't perfection — it's competence. I'm aiming for a level where each form would earn a solid passing grade.

When possible, I plan to have these forms reviewed by qualified instructors from the associations or systems where they originate, for outside feedback and validation.

These videos aren't instructional material or a reference standard — just a record of my own training and progress through this project.

If you're on a similar path, or just interested in forms practice, I hope you find something useful or motivating here. Feel free to reach out at victor dot ronin at gmail dot com.
