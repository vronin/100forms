History

Created by Grandmaster Jae Chul Shin. "Sae Kye" translates to "world," referencing the association's (World Tang Soo Do) name. The Sae Kye Hyung set was built as a kicking-focused counterpart to the older Ki Cho Hyung Il Bu.

Creation date

1987

Associations

World Tang Soo Do Association (WTSDA).
