Chil Sung E Ro

  • Learning
  • Tang Soo Do
  • Open Hand
  • 1st Dan

History

Created by Master Hwang Kee, founder of the Moo Duk Kwan. The forms blend hard striking technique with slower, breath-driven movement, and draw several postures from the Muye Dobo Tongji, a historical Korean military manual. "Chil Sung" translates to "Seven Stars," referencing the stars used to navigate to Polaris, the North Star.

Creation date

1952

Associations

Moo Duk Kwan / Soo Bahk Do and its descendant organizations, as a full or partial set. Not part of the WTSDA curriculum.
