History

Created by Master Hwang Kee, founder of the Moo Duk Kwan. The Ki Cho forms were adapted from the Taikyoku forms of Okinawan karate. "Ki Cho" translates to "basic."

Creation date

1947

Associations

Most Tang Soo Do organizations, except WTSDA.
