History

Created by Master Itosu Yasutsune of Shuri, Okinawa, who built the forms from older kata, later carried to Japan by his student Master Funakoshi Gichin, who renamed them Heian. "Pyung Ahn" translates to "peaceful confidence."

Moo Duk Kwan, under Hwang Kee, once claimed a Chinese origin based on a form called "Jae Nam." His own later writing contradicted this claim.
