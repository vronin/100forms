History

Created by Master Itosu Yasutsune of Shuri, Okinawa, later carried to Japan by his student Master Funakoshi Gichin, who renamed them Heian. "Pyung Ahn" translates to "peaceful confidence."

Historians debate which older kata the forms were built from: one theory points to classical Shuri kata such as Kusanku (Kong Sang Koon) and Passai (Bassai); the other points to a since-lost kata called Channan (Jae Nam), said to have been taught by a Chinese resident of Okinawa.
